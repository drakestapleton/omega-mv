0x800000 -> 0x800800 (the 2048-byte fdtab pool). That merge made descriptor tracking program-local
("VFIO descriptor-kind state exists only in standalone programs", seed/omc/omc.om). embed.om is a
standalone program that calls fclose, so the new bytes are the intended emission, not a change in
embed.om. Every other derive-*/raw-pin/checker-pin case still matches its recorded bytes.
