PROPOSAL (not implemented, not relied on): what contract section 8 for export `final` still needs. L1039, 2026-11-09.
Addressed to coordinator L835 (t_e782e1c3) and Drake. A trust change to a gate: nothing below is in leakscan.om.

Finding (leakscan e14 on the SHA artifact 27775fb2...570b, export final, pub:0:32:8)
All 32 stores that still revoke the count declaration are the digest bytes final writes through its `out`
pointer: words @4729..@4929 (byte offsets 0x49e4..0x4d04), `strb w12,[x1,#0..31]`, x1 = entry slot 1.
Every other poisoning store in final's scan is excused: 235 unreached, 90 below the entry SP, 0 bounded.
The scanner cannot prove slot 1 (out) disjoint from slot 0 (ctx): the unit itself never compares them, so
the DATA bytes may be the count field [32,40) of ctx as far as the artifact shows.

What the contract says (spec/omegese-om2.md section 6, "Outputs"): a parameter declared `NAME out T N` is
written to a binding-owned buffer and copied to the caller's pointer only after status 0. Section 6 also
makes the bridge refuse an `edit` range that overlaps any other array argument. So at the unit's entry the
`out` slot is a binding-owned buffer, disjoint from every caller range. That is a property of omega-bridge's
generated wrapper (native.om), not of the artifact, and no leakscan rule may assume it silently.
Experiment (scratch scanner, one line of rvoutside changed to treat every other-slot store as outside; NOT
committed, NOT a proposal of that rule): final then reports PASS, 0 PUBLIC-MEM-REVOKED, 0 MANUAL branches,
0 data-index (SUMMARY len=91 trap-check=32 data-branch=0 public-index=88 data-index=0). So the 32 `out`
stores are the whole obstacle: the three count-derived MANUAL branches and the one data-index now in
final's report exist only because the declaration is revoked.

Why it also matters to update: the unreached-from-entry rule makes update's PASS conditional on the
all-exports obligation (init, update and final scanned with pub:0:32:8, none revokes). final revokes only
because of the 32 `out` stores, so until final is resolved the obligation is NOT_MET and update's PASS stays
conditional (sha-exports.sh prints this).

Option A (recommended): an explicit caller assertion own:SLOT.
  leakscan ... pub:0:32:8 own:1 e14: "entry slot 1 is a binding-owned out buffer: its bytes are disjoint from
  every other slot's memory". Same trust class as pub: (an incorrect declaration is an unsupported caller
  assertion, printed in the report header as OWN-DECL). It is only usable when the qualifier also records
  the bridge evidence that the generated final wrapper passes a binding-owned buffer (omega-bridge docs/
  NATIVE-ABI.md, tests of "out overlaps ctx" refusal and of the copy-after-status-0 behaviour).
  Effect: the 32 stores are provably outside slot 0; final PASS and obligation MET. Negative fixture needed:
  the same out store with own:1 absent, and own:0 (a ctx declared owned) must not excuse a ctx store.
  Cost: one new CLI word, about 40 lines in leakscan.om, fixtures and mutants as for the other E14 rules.
Option B: flow-sensitive validity plus "final is terminal".
  Per load, revoke only when a poisoning store can execute before it; spec section 7 says the context is
  unspecified after final, so the exit obligation would not cover final. Needs a contract reading (is
  update-after-final without init outside the leakage claim?) and a CFG reachability pass; larger change.
Option C: leave final NOT_RUN. The SHA unit's section 8 verdict stays NOT_RUN with exactly this one item.

Not proposed: treating different entry slots as disjoint in general. Two `view` ranges may overlap by design
(hash(buf,n,buf) passes the same bytes as data and digest; the wrapper's copy-after-status-0 is what keeps
the unit's `out` buffer disjoint), so only a binding-owned out slot qualifies.

Decision needed from L835 / Drake: A, B or C. Until then the recorded result is: init PASS, update PASS
conditional on the open obligation, hash PASS, final NOT_RUN with the 32 named stores.
